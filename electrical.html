Electrical Fundamentals

Welcome to the Electrical Fundamentals course. This course introduces the basic concepts of electrical engineering, including voltage, current, resistance, Ohm's Law, electrical power, and basic circuit analysis. It is designed for beginners who want to build a strong foundation before learning electronics, embedded systems, or PCB design.

Course Objectives

  • Understand Voltage, Current and Resistance.
  • Learn Ohm's Law.
  • Study Series and Parallel Circuits.
  • Understand Kirchhoff's Laws.
  • Calculate Electrical Power.
  • Analyze Basic DC Circuits.

Course Lessons

Lesson 1: Introduction to Electricity
Lesson 2: Voltage, Current & Resistance
Lesson 3: Ohm's Law
Lesson 4: Series & Parallel Circuits
Lesson 5: Kirchhoff's Voltage & Current Laws
Lesson 6: Electrical Power
Lesson 7: Basic Circuit Problems

Learning Outcome

After completing this course, you will understand the fundamentals of electrical engineering and be able to solve basic electrical circuit problems confidently.

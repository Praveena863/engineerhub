2. Introduction

Welcome to Lesson 1. Electricity is the backbone of modern civilization powering industries, homes, and global infrastructure.

3. What is Electricity

Electricity is a form of energy resulting from the existence of charged particles such as electrons or protons.

4. Scientific Definition

Scientifically, electrical current is defined as the rate of flow of electric charge past a point or region per unit time: $I = \frac{dQ}{dt}$.

5. Simple Definition

In simple terms, electricity works just like water moving inside a pressurized pipe.

6. Real-life Explanation

7. Characteristics

8. Interesting Facts

Lesson 1 - Introduction to Electricity

Electricity is the flow of electric charge through a conductor. It is one of the most important forms of energy used in homes, industries, computers, communication systems and electronic devices.

Everything around us such as lights, fans, televisions, mobile phones and laptops works using electrical energy.

Lesson 2 - Voltage, Current & Resistance

Voltage, Current and Resistance are the three basic quantities in every electrical circuit. Understanding these concepts is essential before learning Ohm's Law and circuit analysis.

1. Voltage (V)

Voltage is the electrical pressure that pushes electrons through a conductor.

Unit: Volt (V)
Measured using: Voltmeter
Symbol: V

2. Current (I)

Current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor.

Unit: Ampere (A)
Measured using: Ammeter
Symbol: I

3. Resistance (R)

Resistance is the opposition offered to the flow of electric current.

Unit: Ohm (Ω)
Measured using: Multimeter (Ohm Mode)
Symbol: R

Comparison Table

Quantity Symbol Unit Instrument
Voltage V Volt Voltmeter
Current I Ampere Ammeter
Resistance R Ohm (Ω) Multimeter

Key Points

  • Voltage pushes electrons.
  • Current is the flow of electrons.
  • Resistance opposes current flow.
  • All electrical circuits contain these three quantities.

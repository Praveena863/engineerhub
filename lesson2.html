Lesson 2 : Voltage (Complete Guide)

In this lesson, you will learn everything about Voltage, one of the most important concepts in electrical engineering. After completing this lesson, you will understand what voltage is, how it is produced, where it is used, how to measure it, and how it affects electrical circuits.

1. What is Voltage?

Voltage is the electrical pressure that pushes electrons through a conductor. Without voltage, electrons cannot move. Without electron movement, no current flows. Therefore, voltage is the driving force of every electrical circuit.

Simple Definition: Voltage is the force that pushes electric charges through a wire.

2. Real-Life Example

Imagine a water tank connected to a pipe.

Water System Electrical System
Water Electrons
Water Pressure Voltage
Water Flow Current
Pipe Wire

When water pressure increases, more water flows. Similarly, when voltage increases, electrons get more force to move.

3. Scientific Definition

Voltage is the difference in electric potential between two points. It is also called Potential Difference. The greater the potential difference, the greater the ability to push electrons.

Voltage = Electrical Pressure
